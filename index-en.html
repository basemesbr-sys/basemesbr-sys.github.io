About Me

Who am I?

I am Eng. Basem Esber, a Civil and Structural Engineer with over 20 years of experience in the public sector. Currently, I serve as the Director of Planning at Jableh City Council. I possess a strong background in spatial planning, structural design, project management, and 3D modeling of urban environments.

I am highly passionate about Artificial Intelligence and proficient in Python. I aim to leverage my deep understanding of real-world physical, structural, and spatial environments, combined with technical programming skills, to transition into a Simulation Expert role focused on Real-to-Sim 3D environments.
